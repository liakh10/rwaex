RWAEX DOCS

A cozy island builder on Robinhood Chain. Machines make power, power becomes points, and every payday the pool buys real Stock Tokens and pays the islands

01 · overviewAn island that pays in stocks

Rwaex is a browser game. You claim a floating island with your wallet, build machines on its pads and keep them running. Machines make power; you choose whether power turns into coins for building or into NVDA and GLD points. Every day at 00:00 UTC the reward pool buys real Robinhood Stock Tokens and sends them to the islands that earned points.

The island itself lives on the Rwaex server, so building and collecting cost no gas. Payouts are real token transfers on Robinhood Chain from the payday wallet, each one with a public transaction.

02 · buildVault, pads and storage

Every island starts with a level 1 vault in the centre, a gold mine and 100 coins. The vault opens pads as it levels up and adds offline storage.

├── level 1     4 pads open, 4 hours of storage
├── level 2     6 pads, 6 hours, upgrade for 600 coins
├── level 3     8 pads, 8 hours, upgrade for 2,400 coins
├── storage     machines stop producing once the hours since your last collect run out; collect to start again
├── removing    any machine except the vault, half its price back

04 · keep it runningFuel crates

Derricks need fuel. A free crate arrives every 8 hours. Wallets holding at least 1,000,000 $RWAEX get double crates.

├── crate          10 fuel, 20 for holders
├── every          8 hours
├── tank           30 fuel, +10 per warehouse
├── out of fuel    derricks stop, mines keep digging

05 · chooseWhere power goes

Your route decides what all of the island's power becomes. You can change it once per day; it unlocks again at payday.

Sell to grid
1 coin per power
Stock Press
NVDA points, limited by your presses at 8 per hour each
Bullion Refinery
GLD points, limited by your refineries at 8 per hour each
Overflow
power a converter cannot take is sold to the grid at 25%

Points count on the day you collect them. Collect before 00:00 UTC to have them in that day's payday.

06 · 00:00 UTCPaid in real stocks

After midnight UTC the payday service closes the day and pays it out:

├── pool          the payday wallet balance minus 0.003 ETH kept for gas
├── split         the pool is split between NVDA and GLD in proportion to all eligible points on each route
├── buy NVDA      in the native-ETH Uniswap v4 NVDA pool
├── buy GLD       through Uniswap v3, WETH to USDG to GLD
├── pay           the top 150 islands on each route, pro-rata to their points
├── small pool    under 0.002 ETH it rolls into the next payday

Every payday is listed in the game with its swap transactions, and your own payouts show up with links to each transfer.

07 · fundingWhere the money comes from

$RWAEX launches on Pons with the payday wallet as its creator fee recipient. The payday service claims those fees from the Pons fee escrow on every run and pays them out as stocks. Anyone can also send ETH to the payday wallet to grow a payday. The wallet address is shown in the game.

10 · questionsQuestions

Does building cost gas?No. The game runs on the Rwaex server; only payouts are on-chain.
Why did my island stop?Storage ran out. Open the game and press Collect.
I produced points but got nothingYou need to hold 1,000,000 $RWAEX at payday, be in the top 150 of your route, and the pool must be at least 0.002 ETH.
Can I switch to the press right after picking the grid?Not the same day. The route unlocks at payday.
What are Stock Tokens?Tokenised debt securities issued by Robinhood that track a share price. They are not the shares themselves and are restricted in some jurisdictions, including the US.
